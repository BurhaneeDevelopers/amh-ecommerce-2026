# Product

## Register

product

## Users

**Primary Users:**
- Industrial procurement managers researching and vetting hydraulic component suppliers
- Mechanical and hydraulic engineers seeking specific technical components with precise specifications
- Maintenance supervisors sourcing replacement parts under time pressure
- Business owners comparing authorized dealers for quality assurance

**Context:**
Users arrive with varying urgency levels - from research phase (comparing certifications, authorized brands) to immediate need (equipment down, need quote fast). They value technical accuracy, clear specifications, and fast response times. Trust signals (ISO certifications, authorized dealer status, established since 1999) are critical decision factors.

## Product Purpose

A.M. Hydraulics & Tubes' e-commerce platform serves as both a trust-building brand presence and a lead generation engine. The platform must:

1. **Establish credibility** through ISO certifications, authorized dealer relationships (Parker, Yuken, Rexroth, etc.), and 25+ years of operation
2. **Enable efficient product discovery** with technical specifications, filterable catalogs, and search
3. **Drive quote requests** as the primary conversion goal (not direct purchases)
4. **Facilitate bulk orders** for industrial clients with custom requirements

Success means procurement managers can quickly verify legitimacy, engineers can find exact specifications, and both can request quotes with minimal friction.

## Brand Personality

**Established-Technical-Responsive**

- **Established:** ISO-certified since 1999, authorized by global brands, factory + retail presence
- **Technical:** Precise specifications, engineering-grade data, no marketing fluff
- **Responsive:** Fast quotes, clear contact paths, human expertise available

The brand voice is confident without being corporate-stiff, technical without being intimidating, and service-oriented without being salesy.

## Anti-references

**Avoid:**
- Generic consumer e-commerce aesthetics (Amazon-like, too casual, gamified)
- Overly corporate/sterile B2B portals (gray, lifeless, form-over-function)
- Tech startup trendy (playful illustrations, excessive animations, "disruptive" language)
- Cheap industrial supplier look (cluttered, outdated, low-trust design)
- Marketing buzzword overload ("revolutionize your hydraulics," "next-gen solutions")

**Instead:**
- Industrial-premium: clean, organized, data-forward
- Engineering-focused: specifications are heroes, not afterthoughts
- Trust-first: certifications and authorizations are prominent, not buried

## Design Principles

1. **Specifications are the product:** Technical data tables, dimensions, and compatibility information are primary content, not secondary. Engineers need numbers, not prose.

2. **Trust through proof, not claims:** ISO badges, authorized dealer logos, factory photos, and contact details build credibility. Avoid empty "world-class" or "leading provider" language.

3. **Friction-free quote requests:** Every product page should make "Get a Quote" the clearest path. Bulk quote flows should feel purpose-built, not generic contact forms.

4. **Dual-mode design:** About Us leans brand-authoritative (certifications, history, leadership). Product pages lean conversion-focused (specs, CTAs, urgency). Both share the same visual system but different content hierarchies.

5. **Mobile-ready for field use:** Engineers and maintenance staff may browse on-site. Touch targets, readable specs on small screens, and fast load times are non-negotiable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **WCAG 2.1 AA compliance** minimum (body text 4.5:1 contrast, interactive elements 3:1)
- **Keyboard navigation** for all interactive elements (engineers often use desktop workflows)
- **Reduced motion support** for animations (industrial users may have vestibular sensitivities)
- **Screen reader compatibility** for specification tables and product data
- **Multi-language consideration** (future: Tamil, Hindi for local Chennai market)
